the small model reads one token and predicts the token that follows.
the model begins with random numbers and learns from repeated examples.
when a prediction is wrong the loss is large.
when a prediction improves the loss becomes smaller.
training changes the parameters a little at every step.
the tokenizer converts text into integer identifiers.
the batch builder creates inputs and their next token targets.
the model converts each input token into scores for every possible next token.
softmax converts the scores into probabilities.
cross entropy measures how much probability the model gave the correct token.
the optimizer uses gradients to update the model parameters.
generation samples one new token and then repeats the same process.
testing checks that every component obeys its mathematical contract.
measurement tells us whether an optimization actually improved the system.
research begins with a clear question and a reproducible baseline.
